Reports closed upper bounds (#maxIncl) and open lower bounds (#minExcl) on discrete numeric types to enforce half-open interval governance (STVN Spec § 3.2).

Discrete Half-Open Interval Invariant (ERR_DISCRETE_BOUND_KIND_PROHIBITED):

  • Discrete numeric domains (:Int and { #exact } :Float) require half-open intervals [minIncl, maxExcl).
  • Closed upper bound #maxIncl <N> is rejected; use half-open bound #maxExcl <N+1> instead.
  • Open lower bound #minExcl <N> is rejected; use half-open bound #minIncl <N+1> instead.

Provides automated intention quick-fixes to convert bounds to canonical half-open form.
