Verifies that document metadata declarations for #name and #domain facets in root :meta match the filename stem hierarchy.

Filename stem slot 0 corresponds to the declared document #name. Filename stem slot 1 corresponds to the optional declared document #domain.

Reports WARN_DOCUMENT_NAME_MISMATCH (W001) when declared #name or #domain differs from its filename stem slot, WARN_DOCUMENT_DOMAIN_OMITTED_IN_FILENAME or WARN_DOCUMENT_DOMAIN_OMITTED_IN_META (W002) when only one side declares a domain, and ERR_RESERVED_DOMAIN_PROHIBITED (E038) when a user document declares a reserved stvn_* domain.

Quick-Fix:

Aligns the #name or #domain facet with filename stem slot 0 or slot 1.

Renames the file to match a valid non-reserved declared #name or #domain.

Removes the #domain facet on 1-stem filenames or strips the reserved stvn_ prefix from #domain.
