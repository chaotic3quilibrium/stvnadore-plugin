Audits STVN document source to enforce the Strict Zero-Tab Invariant (STVN Spec § 1.3).

Raw horizontal tab characters (\t, U+0009) are forbidden outside Rule STR-04 verbatim fenced string bodies ("""[TAG] ... [TAG]""", STVN Spec § 5.3). Only standard ASCII space characters (U+0020) are permitted for structural indentation and delimiter alignment.

Quick-Fix:

Replaces the offending structural tab character with two standard ASCII spaces.
