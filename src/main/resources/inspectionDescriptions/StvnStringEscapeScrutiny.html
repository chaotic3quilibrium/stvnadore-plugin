Reports valid escape sequences inside double-quoted STVN simple strings ("...") that decode to hazardous control, bidirectional text-direction, or invisible format scalars, and optionally flags shorthand \r carriage-return escapes.

  • Hazardous Control, Bidirectional & Invisible Escapes (PW007): Warns on escaped C0 controls (excluding \t, \n, and \r), DEL (U+007F), C1 controls (U+0080–U+009F), bidirectional override and isolate scalars (Trojan Source), and invisible format or line-separator scalars (U+200B, U+2060, U+2028, U+2029, U+FEFF).
  • Shorthand \r Escape Scrutiny (PW008, enabled by default): Warns on 2-character shorthand \r (U+000D CARRIAGE RETURN), which cannot be represented verbatim in Rule STR-04 fenced strings ("""[TAG]...[TAG]"""). Use the Replace '\r' with explicit '\u000D' quick-fix when deliberate carriage-return retention is required.
  • Clean Exemptions: Explicit \u000D / \u000d, emoji joiners (\u200C, \u200D), standard escapes (\n, \t, \", \\, \/), and printable Unicode escapes (such as \u0041, \u2705, and \uD83D\uDE80) produce zero warnings.

Configure the Warn on shorthand \r escape (use explicit \u000D when intentional) checkbox in the inspection options panel to toggle shorthand \r scrutiny independently.
